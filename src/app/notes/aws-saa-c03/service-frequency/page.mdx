## Takeaways

- **Compute and Storage carry the exam.** EC2 (478) and S3 (401) together account for over a third of all service mentions; combined with Lambda, RDS, Auto Scaling, VPC, IAM, DynamoDB, CloudFront, and EBS, the Top 10 alone covers the majority.
- **Categories worth a deeper read:** Networking and Databases are well-distributed — multiple services within each category appear frequently, so don't stop at the leader.
- **Categories you can skim:** Developer Tools (only X-Ray shows up), IoT (zero), and most of ML & AI rarely appear. Spend minimal time here.
- **Watch for zeros.** Bedrock, Personalize, Keyspaces, MemoryDB, OpsWorks, License Manager, and the entire CodeStar suite are essentially absent — recognize them by name but don't memorize their details.
