# IV Tier

![[2025-08-10_23.56.43.png]]

From the IV tier onward, the difficulty ramps up sharply. This is the point where players must face the Platinum Processing Line, a bottleneck where many quit. This tier also introduces many multiblock machines, so the whole base needed to be reworked.

---

## Power Generation

### Generators

Originally, the base was powered by cetane-boosted diesel, but crop production could not scale as easily as benzene. Currently, a Large Gas Turbine handles normal loads, while the LCE is switched on when extra power is needed.  
![[2025-08-10_23.51.56.png]]

### Benzene Production

- 11×11 EnderIO Tree Farm
- Coke Oven → Fluid Extractor → 3× Distillation Towers
- Output: 1,200 L/s benzene (HSS-E Tight LGT burns 260 L/s)

## Polybenzimidazole (PBI)

Using a relatively simple chemical process, I made polybenzimidazole, a material essential for space exploration. I didn't make a whole production chain for it, just a on-demand AE auto-crafting to get things started.

## Tier-3 Rocket and Ceres

Tier 3 space exploration takes 3 things:
1. Rocket
2. Fuel
3. Thermal Clothing

The tier 3 rocket is just a bunch of desh and titanium, which is quite simple at this point.

![[2026-04-25_15.12.14.png]]

The tier 3 rocket fuel "Dense Hydrazine Fuel"'s ingredient is also simple. However processing it takes a Tier-5 chemical plant, which took out my whole titanium stock.

![[2026-04-25_15.11.43.png]]

The new thermal clothing requires PBI to make, which we have done in advance.

![[Pasted image 20260425151710.png]]

Finally, I landed on Ceres and obtained Naquada to progress.

## Assemblyline

The assembly casing is stupidly expensive, each costs 6 most advanced circuits at IV and takes hours to craft.

![[Pasted image 20260425150027.png]]

While most of it is still crafting, I craft the controller block and it grant me a trophy and access to Luv Quest.

![[Pasted image 20260425150421.png]]
![[Pasted image 20260425150835.png]]

And 1 day after the AE finish crafting the remaining assembly casing.
![[Screenshot 2026-04-26 153803.png]]

I set up the AE automation for Ass Line by following [Cat's Cozy Games tutorial](https://youtu.be/nUO69RjOCcs?si=DpJqSrVigrglNnCX), its basicly the same as multiblock AE automation, with the addition of priority and naming. 
![[Screenshot 2026-05-14 220825.png]]
And after all these, I realise I need Samarium to do any ass line crafting, which needs yet another tedious chem line and a few new multiblocks. I fully understand why IV is the hardest line now, trouble just comes one after another.
