# LUV Tier

Starting from LUV, the playstyle will change drastically, and we will replace most single-block machines with large multiblocks and work on our first fusion reactor.

## Pre-LUV

### QoL Tools
I made a bunch of QoL tools. They are: 
![[Screenshot 2026-05-15 102345.png]]

1. Traveller's Tool Belt: provide an extra hotbar
2. Universal Wireless Terminal: All-in-one AE Terminal

4. Quantum suit
5. Wireless Charger MK IV: Wireless charging with 8km range
