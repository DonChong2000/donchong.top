## Steam Age to MV Tier

We chose the Canyon biome for our base because of its unique view, lack of rain, high blast resistance, and most importantly, no stone or cobblestone, which prevents the dreaded "drown creeper" (a creeper variant that spawns swarms of silverfish that hide in stone and multiply).
